Carbon Responder coordinates demand response across heterogeneous datacenter workloads, balancing carbon reduction with performance and fairness. It supports centralized and decentralized policies for allocating power curtailment across online and batch services.

[Read the paper on arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.08589).

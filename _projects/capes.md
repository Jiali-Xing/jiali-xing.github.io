CAPES applies regression discontinuity design to isolate the causal effects of score-based scheduling decisions on server power and LLM-serving latency. The framework helps distinguish the scheduler's impact from workload variation and other confounding factors in cloud clusters.

[Read the AIOps '26 paper](https://cloudintelligenceworkshop.org/papers/aiops26-Xing.pdf).
